06→Questions
Do we need to replace our case management software?

No. Overclerk builds connect to the tools you already use, such as Clio, MyCase, Filevine, Microsoft 365 and your phone system. If a tool has no way to connect, we'll tell you in the audit.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong?

Every build includes human review at the points that matter. The system drafts and flags; your team confirms. Records and actions are logged, so you can see exactly what happened and correct it.

What does it cost?

The audit is free. Builds are fixed-price and start from $10,000, depending on how many systems are involved and how complex the workflow is. You'll have the exact price in writing before any work starts.

Will this replace our staff?

It takes repetitive typing and filing off their plate, so assistants and paralegals spend their time on work that needs judgement. Most firms use it to handle growth without adding headcount.

How long does a build take?

It depends on scope. A single workflow is usually measured in weeks, not months, and you'll get a timeline in the proposal.
